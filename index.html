Preface

Languages are constantly growing and evolving, and it seems we are using fewer and fewer words to communicate. As an English as a Second Language (ESL) instructor with more than 15 years of experience, I have seen how students can become overwhelmed by the many acronyms they encounter every day. I've had the privilege of working with talented educators in many places, near and far. We all share the same goal: helping our students become fluent and confident English speakers. This book was written to help English learners better understand the acronyms they encounter in everyday life.
